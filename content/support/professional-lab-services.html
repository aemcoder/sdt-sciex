Overview

Expertise and professionalism your lab can rely on.

The SCIEX professional services team draws upon decades of experience from all aspects of life science workflows and laboratory environments. There are lots of key projects that the SCIEX Professional Services team can help you with to ensure your lab increases productivity, compliance attainment, instrument verification and relocation. Regardless of the task in hand the SCIEX Professional Service experts are your trusted partners in delivering laboratory and scientific excellence.

Customization

One size does not fit all. SCIEX professional services understands that all labs and workflows are different. Standards and regulations vary by region and updates may need to be applied to a system where all activities must be documented in a SOP record. You can trust that the team will bring the same level of support and commitment to a first-time request as to a reoccurring common procedure.

Experts

The SCIEX professional services consists of an extensive team of industry and system specialists who are focused on understanding and addressing the challenges you face and providing a clear path to a robust solution. Our expertise covers workflows, instrument configurations, method validations, compliance and more.

Budget

We understand that budgets are not infinite. The services and products we deliver will be sized accordinging to your labs needs and available budget. Timelines, can be sensitive and certain projects may need expediting. During the consultation phase of your project, the team will work within your parameters to deliver the best tailored solution within your financial and time considerations.

Coverage

SCIEX professional services are not limited to mass spectrometry workflows. The team has proven experience across the entire SCIEX portfolio from capillary electrophoresis and software, including delivering a seamless transition from Analyst software to SCIEX OS software.

Bespoke solutions for your lab's needs

Laboratories are unique, there is not one set of services that can comprehensively support requirements, objectives and goals. The SCIEX professional services team offer customized solutions and services to help realise your lab’s ambition. The team offer the following:

  • Full consultation and evaluation of your needs
  • Improved efficiencies and increased productivity
  • Complete visibility into financial considerations for cost reduction
  • Resource support


As the team find out more about your needs and the scope of the work required you will receive a project pan with costings aligned to your budgets, along with an expected timeline.
