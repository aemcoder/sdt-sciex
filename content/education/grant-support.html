Helping make your research possible

Our team is here to help you leverage grant opportunities so that your lab can pursue the resources it needs. SCIEX believes in connecting scientists to research and funding tools that empower you to make your next discovery. That starts here.

  • Make decisions about your lab’s future with the latest content to support your application.
  • Connect with a team of scientific experts to refine and submit your grant proposal.
  • Expand your COVID-19 grant research — we can help show you the top funding opportunities within your industry.
  • Get your lab off to a great start with our academic packages, which are available in three diverse coverage options.

The SCIEX team is here for you along the way. Receive grant application information for your industry when you contact our team.
