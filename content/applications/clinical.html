Applications from biomarker discovery to verified diagnostic test

It is vital for clinical laboratory scientists—from healthcare innovators pursuing clinical research to those performing frontline diagnostics in hospital testing labs—to have affordable, reliable and robust technologies that deliver the results with the utmost accuracy as quickly as possible. However, many busy clinical laboratories have settled for the undesirable compromise of costly send-out testing, as cutting-edge technologies have previously been unobtainable. The implication is high outsourcing expenditure, extended turnaround times and compromises on assay performance, all of which directly influence patient care.

SCIEX is the ideal partner for addressing these concerns and helping your modern clinical biochemistry laboratory meet its analytical goals now and in the future. We provide a strong portfolio of mass spectrometers, workflow solutions, software, aftercare and third party relationships to meet and exceed your daily needs. In addition, as next-generation research into disease and medicine dictates the needs of tomorrow, SCIEX robust, sensitive, and easy-to-use systems, such as the Citrine system and the 4500MD system, support the entire clinical laboratory pipeline, from research to the clinic. From discovering and validating new biomarkers to executing end-to-end validated workflows for high-throughput testing in the clinic, we are trusted by scientists to advance their work in clinical research and diagnostics.
