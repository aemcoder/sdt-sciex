PEAKS

Built on industry-leading de novo sequencing enhanced by deep learning, PEAKS uniquely integrates spectral library and database search, PTM and sequence variant discovery, and robust quantification in a single, streamlined workflow - enabling researchers to uncover novel biology, maximize proteome coverage, and trust every result, whether working with IDA/DDA, Zeno SWATH or ZT Scan DIA, or targeted proteomics data.

MS-DIAL

Open‑source software for untargeted metabolomics and lipidomics, supporting peak detection, deconvolution, compound annotation, and statistical analysis in complex biological datasets. Optimized for SCIEX SWATH, ZT Scan DIA and EAD workflows, enabling improved precursor–fragment association and greater structural confidence for small‑molecule discovery.

SKYLINE

A freely available and open-source Windows client application that allows you to process SCIEX raw data directly for building Selected Reaction Monitoring (SRM) / Multiple Reaction Monitoring (MRM), Parallel Reaction Monitoring (PRM), Data Independent Acquisition (DIA/SWATH), and DDA with MS1 quantitative methods and analyze the resulting mass spectrometer data. Its flexible configuration supports all molecules. It aims to employ cutting-edge technologies to create and iteratively refine targeted methods for large-scale quantitative mass spectrometry studies in life sciences.
