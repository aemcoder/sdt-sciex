- **`lead` disc lists** (KC): `ul` sits flush under its paragraph (live `.richtext ul` has no padding-top) → `margin-top: -gap`.
- **Video split**: player column keeps the live Splide pager margin under it (12/16 px) so the section measures 481 as live; `columns split cover` (NEW modifier) crops the forensics split-right picture (1500×840 asset) to the live 4:3 frame.
- **`columns lead`**: CTA paragraphs flow as a wrapping row (live `.atomic-buttons`), the heading + lede sit flush (`big-lede` negative gap), single flex line packed at the start (no edit-mode stretch drift).
- **Accent arrow**: live `.btn-secondary` svg has `margin-left: 0` (the primary's is 16) → flush arrow in `lead` / `columns lead` contexts (G3's global accent keeps 16 for its /stories pills).
- **Statement word geometry** (live spans: "Thought" post-rotation 0..234, "leaders" 140..346 in a 128×346 box) reproduced with `text-indent: 112px` (counted in the min-content line) + `text-align-last: start` — measured glyph rects identical to live (x54 0..234 / x118 140..346).
